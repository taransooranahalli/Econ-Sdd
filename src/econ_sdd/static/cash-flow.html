Input conventions & calculation details

Net cash flow is monthly income minus expenses. A surplus is available before savings allocations; it is not a bank balance or a forecast. Monthly totals do not show whether cash runs short between paydays.

Enter take-home income after deductions. Add applicable bills, fees, tax and debt payments yourself. Exclude transfers to your own savings or investment accounts. If you count card purchases, exclude repayments of those same purchases to avoid double-counting.

No frequency conversion, tax calculation, interest, inflation, or exchange-rate model. Percentage shares are rounded independently and may not sum to exactly 100%.
